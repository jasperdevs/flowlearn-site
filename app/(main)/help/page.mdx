## Help & FAQ

Everything you need to get the most out of FlowLearn. Still stuck? Email us at
[hello@flowlearn.app](mailto:hello@flowlearn.app) and we'll help.

### Getting started

**What is FlowLearn?**
FlowLearn is an iPhone app for learning anything in short, focused sessions. You
can take ready-made courses or describe any topic and let FlowLearn build a lesson
for you, then keep it in memory with spaced recall.

**How do I make my own lesson?**
On the home screen, tap **Make your own lesson**, choose a subject, and describe
what you want to learn. FlowLearn writes a structured lesson you can start right
away.

### Streaks, XP & progress

**How do streaks work?**
Complete at least one lesson a day to keep your streak alive. Daily goals and
optional reminders help you build the habit.

**What is XP and what are levels?**
You earn XP for completing lessons and quizzes. XP raises your level and your
place on the leaderboard.

### Friends & invites

**How do I add friends?**
Open your profile and tap **Add friends**, or share your invite QR code. When
someone scans your code or opens your invite link, they can download FlowLearn and
connect with you.

**Someone sent me an invite link.**
Open the link on your iPhone. If you already have FlowLearn, it opens straight to
the invite. If not, you'll be taken to download it first, then enter the code.

### Account & data

**Does FlowLearn sync across devices?**
Yes. Your progress syncs through your private iCloud account. Make sure you're
signed in to iCloud on each device.

**How do I delete my account?**
Go to **Settings → Delete account** in the app. This removes your account and the
data we store in the cloud.

### FlowLearn Plus

**What's included?**
FlowLearn Plus unlocks the full experience, including unlimited AI-built lessons.
You can manage or cancel anytime from **Settings → Manage subscription**, which
opens your App Store subscriptions.
