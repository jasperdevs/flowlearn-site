## Privacy Policy

_Last updated: June 10, 2026_

FlowLearn is built to be calm, focused, and respectful of your data. This policy
explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

### What we collect

- **Your profile.** A display name and avatar you choose, plus optional settings
  like your daily goal and appearance.
- **Learning activity.** Your lessons, progress, streaks, XP, and which topics
  you study, so we can sync your progress and personalize what you see.
- **Content you create.** Topics and prompts you give FlowLearn to generate
  lessons.
- **Diagnostics.** Basic, aggregated app performance and crash information to keep
  FlowLearn stable.

We do **not** sell your personal data, and we don't use it to build advertising
profiles.

### How your data is stored

Your progress is stored on your device and synced through your private **iCloud**
account using Apple's CloudKit. We don't have access to your iCloud credentials.

### AI features

When you generate a lesson, the topic or prompt you provide is sent to our
processing service (and the AI providers powering it) solely to produce that
lesson. We don't use your prompts to train third-party models beyond what's
needed to return your result.

### Subscriptions

Payments and subscription status are handled by the Apple App Store and our
subscription provider (Adapty). We receive your subscription status, not your
payment details.

### Friends and sharing

If you invite friends or share your profile (for example via a QR code or invite
link), the link contains an invite code or your chosen handle so the recipient can
connect with you. You control whether to share these.

### Your choices

- Edit or remove your profile details at any time in the app.
- Turn off reminders, sounds, and haptics in Settings.
- Delete your account and associated cloud data from **Settings → Delete account**.

### Children

FlowLearn is intended for general audiences. We don't knowingly collect personal
information from children where prohibited by law. If you believe a child has
provided us personal information, contact us and we'll remove it.

### Changes

We may update this policy from time to time and will revise the date above when we
do.
